Top Travel Destinations in Yemen 🇾🇪

Ibb Greenery

Known as the "Green Province," Ibb offers breathtaking landscapes and mountains covered in green during the rainy season.

Hadramaut Heritage

Discover the ancient architecture and the deep history of the desert valleys and the unique mud-brick buildings of Hadramaut.

Sana’a Old City

A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring unique decorated houses and traditional markets that date back thousands of years.

Socotra Island

Explore the most alien-looking place on Earth with its rare Dragon Blood trees and pristine white sand beaches.

Bab al-Yaman

The historic main gate to the old city of Sana'a, standing as a symbol of Yemeni strength and ancient architectural art.

Great Marib Dam

A masterpiece of ancient engineering and a witness to the greatness of the Sabaean kingdom and Yemen's hydraulic history.
