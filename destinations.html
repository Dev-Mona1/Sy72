The Most Beautiful Places in Yemen

Discover extraordinary destinations that showcase Yemen's unparalleled heritage and nature

Yemen is home to some of the world's most breathtaking destinations — from ancient skyscraper cities to alien-like islands. Each place tells a story spanning thousands of years of civilization and natural wonder.

🏛

Old Sana'a

CAPITAL · UNESCO SITE
🏅 UNESCO World Heritage

One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities. Famous for its unique tower-house architecture adorned with geometric alabaster windows called "qamariyyas" — a sight unlike anywhere else on Earth.

Shibam Hadramaut

HADHRAMAUT · UNESCO SITE
🏅 UNESCO World Heritage

Known as the "Manhattan of the Desert" — a 16th-century city of towering mud-brick skyscrapers rising from the valley floor. An extraordinary feat of pre-modern engineering and human ingenuity.

Socotra Island

ARABIAN SEA · UNESCO SITE
🏅 UNESCO World Heritage

Called the "Galápagos of the Indian Ocean" — Socotra is home to over 700 species found nowhere else on Earth, including the iconic Dragon Blood Tree with its umbrella-shaped canopy and crimson sap.

Mountainous Ibb

SOUTHWESTERN YEMEN

Yemen's greenest governorate — nicknamed the "Green Province." Ibb's fertile mountains, dramatic terraced fields, cool climate, and cascading waterfalls offer a lush contrast to Yemen's desert landscapes.

Bab al-Yemen

SANA'A · HISTORIC GATE

The iconic gateway to the Old City of Sana'a. Standing for over a millennium, it serves as a grand entrance to a world of bustling traditional markets (Souqs) and mesmerizing historic architecture.

Marib Dam

MARIB · ANCIENT ENGINEERING

A masterpiece of ancient engineering. The Great Dam of Marib dates back to the 8th century BC, once supporting the legendary Kingdom of Saba and symbolizing Yemen's agricultural ingenuity.
