About Cyber Sheba

A digital project reviving Yemeni heritage through modern technology

Who Are We?

Cyber Sheba is a digital project aimed at highlighting the hidden beauty of Yemen, presenting its ancient heritage and stunning tourist landmarks in a modern and attractive style worthy of this great human legacy.

We believe that Yemen holds a wealth of beauty and civilization that the world deserves to know; from the Old City of Sana'a to the magical Socotra Island, passing through Shibam Hadramout and the green mountains of Taiz.

This project is an intersection of passion for heritage and technical skill, where code and design meet to serve a noble cause: painting a bright and vivid image of "Happy Yemen."
